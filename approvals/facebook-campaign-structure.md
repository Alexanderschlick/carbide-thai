## 7-Day Test KPIs

After 7 days, evaluate:

| Metric | Target | Action if missed |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| CPM | < ฿80 | Broaden audience |
| CTR | > 2% | Refresh creative |
| Cost per Lead | < ฿200 | Adjust targeting |
| Cost per Contact (LINE) | < ฿150 | Check LINE CTA placement |

Scale winner to ฿500/day after 7 days if CPA looks good.
