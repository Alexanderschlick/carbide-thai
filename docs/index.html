Logistics · Delivery Options
Getting Your Scrap to Us
📦 OPTION 1 — NATIONWIDE

Ship to Us by Courier

Pack your carbide securely and send via any Thai courier — Kerry Express, J&T, Flash, Thailand Post. All work fine for carbide.

Our full receiving address is sent to you via LINE after you submit your order form. We do not publish the address publicly.

Recommended: double-box heavy items over 5kg. We cover return shipping if there's an issue.

🚚 OPTION 2 — GREATER BANGKOK & EASTERN SEABOARD

We Pick Up From You

For larger quantities (20kg+) in Bangkok, Chonburi, or Rayong, we can arrange a pickup directly from your factory or workshop.

Request pickup in the order form and we'll coordinate a time that works for you.

Pickup available Mon–Fri. Minimum 20kg for pickup service.
